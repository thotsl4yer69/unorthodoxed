Origin / Manifesto
Melbourne Est. 2024 Hand Numbered No Restocks

Born in Melbourne's underground.

No mass production. No compromise.

Built for those who refuse the algorithm.

Every drop is a statement. Limited runs of 100 pieces or fewer, hand-numbered, screen-printed in the warehouse district. We don't restock. We don't follow trends. We work with three printers, two designers, and one rule: if it isn't worth saying loud, we don't make it. This is not a brand. This is a refusal — of fast fashion, of algorithmic taste, of the polite uniform of the agreeable. Wear it because you mean it.
